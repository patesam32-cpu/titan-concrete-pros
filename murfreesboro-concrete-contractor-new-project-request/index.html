Built for Rutherford County Ground

Murfreesboro sits on clay that swells and shrinks, with limestone close to the surface, freeze-thaw winters and around 54 inches of rain a year. Thin slabs poured on dirt crack here. This is how we pour instead.

Why So Many Murfreesboro Driveways Crack

Most failing driveways here weren't poured badly on purpose. They were poured 4 in. thick on bare clay, with no compacted base and joints too far apart. Clay moves, water sits under the slab, winter freezes it, and panels crack and sink.

Sometimes a section repair is enough. When the ground under the slab has failed, the lasting fix is a new slab built on stone, at the right thickness, with joints where cracks belong.

Questions Before You Pour

How much does a concrete driveway cost in Murfreesboro?

A new 5 in. driveway in Murfreesboro typically runs $8 to $12 per sq ft, or about $4,000 to $7,200 for a two-car driveway. Replacing an old one adds tear-out, usually $9.50 to $13.50 per sq ft all in. Your written quote after a free visit sets the real number.

Do I need a permit?

Often, yes. Work where your driveway meets a city street usually needs a city right-of-way permit, state highways need a TDOT permit too, and slabs for garages, shops and additions need a building permit. Ground-level patios usually don't. We tell you at the visit and pull whatever your job needs.

Can you pour in winter?

Yes, on days when the forecast allows, using cold-weather practices like insulated blankets and an adjusted mix. Many people book their estimate in winter to lock in an early spring pour date.

When can I drive on my new driveway?

You can usually walk on it after a day or two and drive a car on it after about a week. Heavy trucks and RVs should wait about four weeks while the concrete reaches full strength.

Will it crack?

All concrete can crack; control joints decide where. A compacted stone base, the right thickness, an air-entrained mix and joints at the right spacing keep cracks small and in the joints.

Do you handle HOA approval?

Yes. We prepare the drawing and spec sheet most Murfreesboro HOAs ask for before you pour a driveway, patio or walkway.

Do you repair concrete, or only replace it?

Both. If a cracked or sunken section can be fixed so it lasts, we repair it. If the ground under the slab has failed, we show you why replacing it is the better spend. You get our honest read at the free visit.
